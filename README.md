# Petal

A cosy 2D florist game for Android and iOS. Grow flowers in your greenhouse, turn them into bouquets for the
people of a little town, and spend what you earn on upgrades, new seeds and shop themes. 

Built with Godot 4.7 (C#).

## Done

**Greenhouse**

- Six pots to plant, water, fertilize, harvest and clear.
- Plants grow over days, die if they go unwatered and wither if they aren't harvested in time.
- Growth and Bloom fertilizers, harvest quality in stars.
- Garden supplies cabinet for buying seeds (choosing the colour) and fertilizer.
- Today's inventory of harvested flowers.
- Hold a pot to see what's growing in it, or hold a tool to choose which seed or fertilizer it uses.
- **Open Shop** ends the day for now (the shop itself doesn't exist yet).

**Also**

- Main menu and settings (language).
- English and Czech.

## Planned

- **Save slots:** three slots with an autosave at the start of each day.
- **Shop:** townsfolk come in with requests, from "five red roses" to "surprise me". You accept or decline them
  and craft bouquets with wraps and decorations. Customers pay you based on how satisfied they are.
- **Evening summary:** customers served, money earned, new journal entries.
- **Upgrade shop:** extra plots, sprinklers, a heater for rare flowers, a cold display fridge, seed catalogs and
  shop themes.
- **Long-term play:** advance orders, a flower journal to complete, and an album of favourite bouquets.
- **Tutorial day**, which teaches the player how to play.
- **Final art:** the current SVG graphics are placeholders.
